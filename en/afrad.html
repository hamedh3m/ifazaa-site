What Fazaa does for you

Everything in everyday language, no commands or menus.

  • Saves appointments and reminds you before

    Say how early you want the reminder, or let it pick a sensible time.

    “Dentist Sunday at 4pm, remind me an hour before”
  • Remembers yearly occasions

    Birthdays and anniversaries, every year, on its own.

    “Mom's birthday is March 12, remind me every year”
  • Daily and weekly reminders

    Medication, exercise, paying a bill. Stop it with a word.

    “Remind me to take my blood-pressure pill every day at 10am”
  • Writes your messages and greetings

    Congratulations, condolences, an apology, or a formal message, ready to copy and send yourself.

    “Write a graduation message for my nephew”
  • Answers and helps you decide

    Ask it anything, or have it plan your week or a shopping list.

    “Plan my Saturday: work, gym, and visiting my family”
  • Searches the web for you

    Prices, restaurants, opening hours, government procedures, product comparisons. It looks and replies with the summary and the source.

    “Find the cheapest iPhone 17 price in Jeddah”
  • Writes your emails

    Emails for work, school, or a company, in Arabic or English, ready to copy and send yourself.

    “Write an email to my manager asking for Sunday off”
  • Helps with design ideas

    Writes the text for an invitation or poster and suggests colors and layout, for your designer or to make yourself.

    “I need an idea for my daughter's birthday party invitation”
  • Private setup for you

    Want it on your own WhatsApp number, or connected to your email, calendar, and smart home? We set it up for SAR 1,490 one-time, with optional support at SAR 490 a year, half price for the first 10 subscribers.

    “I want my own assistant on my number”
  • Gives you your day and week

    Ask what's coming up and get a tidy list, then cancel or change anything.

    “What's on this week? And cancel Sunday's appointment”
