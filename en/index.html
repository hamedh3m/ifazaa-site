Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between yearly and buying once?

With the yearly plan we cover everything: running costs, WhatsApp messages, and AI, with no other charges all year. When you buy once, the device is yours, but WhatsApp messages and AI are billed to you monthly based on your number of conversations, and you pay them directly.

Does it answer my customers without me knowing?

It only answers with the information you give us. Anything it doesn't know, and any complaint, comes to you, and you see everything in the morning report.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

Either a new number just for Fazaa, or we connect your current WhatsApp Business number and you keep using the app on it.

Does it speak English?

Yes. Write in English and it replies in English; write in Arabic and it replies in Saudi dialect. Talk to it like you would to a colleague: "Close pitch 2 tomorrow", "How many bookings Thursday?", "Remind me at 4".

Does it send anything in my name?

It answers your business's customers at your request. Any other email or message it drafts for you, and you send it yourself.
