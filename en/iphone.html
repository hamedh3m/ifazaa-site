Get Fazaa on your iPhone

Fazaa is coming soon to the App Store. Until then, you can install it today from TestFlight, Apple's official app for beta versions. It's free and takes two minutes.

1 Get TestFlight 2 Open Fazaa invite
On a computer?

Scan the code with your iPhone camera to open this page on your phone.

Step by step

  1. Install TestFlight

    A free app from Apple itself, used to install beta versions of apps before they reach the store.

    TestFlight on the App Store

  2. Open the Fazaa invite

    Tap the button on your iPhone and TestFlight opens straight to Fazaa.

    Open Fazaa invite

  3. Tap “Accept”, then “Install”

    The first time you accept the invite, then install. It only takes a few seconds.

    AcceptInstall

  4. Open Fazaa and sign in

    Use the same Apple or Google account you use with Fazaa, and your chats and reminders are right there.

Don't want to install anything? Fazaa works in your browser too: open Fazaa here.

Quick questions

What is TestFlight?

Apple's official way to try apps before they reach the App Store. It's the same Fazaa you know, delivered through TestFlight instead of the store.

Is it free?

Yes, TestFlight and Fazaa are free to install. Plans are the same as on the website, including a free plan forever.

Which iPhones are supported?

Any iPhone on iOS 15 or later.

How do updates work?

TestFlight tells you when a new version is out and you update with one tap. Each beta runs for 90 days and a new one arrives before it ends. Once Fazaa is on the App Store, install it from there.

It says “This beta is full” or the invite isn't available

Beta seats are temporarily full. Try again in a day, and use Fazaa in your browser meanwhile.

What about my data?

Same account and same privacy policy, in the app or the browser. Details in our privacy policy.
