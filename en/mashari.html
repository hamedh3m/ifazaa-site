What it does for your business

Reception talks to your customers, and the secretary works for you. A good fit for sports courts and gyms, salons, restaurants and cafés, clinics, and laundries.

  • Reception: answers customers 24/7

    With your prices, hours, location, and offers, even after you close.

    “How much is an hour on the pitch? Anything free tomorrow at 9?”
  • Reception: books and reminds

    Books or sends the booking link, and reminds customers before their appointment so they show up.

    “I'd like a slot tomorrow afternoon for two”
  • Reception: passes you what needs you

    Complaints and special requests reach you right away, with a summary.

    “I have some feedback about yesterday's service”
  • Secretary: a report every morning

    Bookings, income, free slots, and who's waiting on you.

    “Good morning: 18 bookings yesterday, and 2 waiting on you”
  • Secretary: follows your instructions in plain words

    Block a time, change a price, ask about bookings, without opening any system.

    “Close pitch 2 tomorrow from 5 to 7”
  • Secretary: follows up with your staff

    Reminds each person of their task, and tells you if someone falls behind.

    “Remind Fahad to order new balls before Thursday”
  • Secretary: writes for you

    Supplier emails, offers, and replies to Google Maps reviews. You review and send them.

    “Write an email to the supplier ordering 20 cases of water”
  • Answers from your business's WhatsApp number

    A new number just for Fazaa, or your current WhatsApp Business number while you keep using the app on it. We explain the options before we start.

    “I want replies to come from my own business number”

Frequently asked questions

Does it understand English and Arabic?

Yes. Talk to it like a friend: "Remind me at 4", "What's on tomorrow?". Write in English and it replies in English, and it understands voice notes too.

Does it send anything in my name?

No. Any email or message it drafts for you, and you send it yourself.

What happens to my data?

It's yours alone. We don't sell it or share it with anyone, and you can delete all of it anytime by saying "delete my data".
