Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Fazaa is a personal assistant on Telegram. This page explains what data we keep, why, and how to delete it.

What we keep

  • Your Telegram chat ID and display name.
  • Your messages to Fazaa and its replies, so it understands you in the context of the conversation.
  • The appointments, occasions, and reminders you ask it to save.
  • Your message count, your trial start date, and your language (Arabic or English), for plan limits, the free trial, and replying in your language.

Why we keep it

Only to serve you: to understand your request, save your appointments, and send your reminders on time. We don't sell your data, use it for ads, or share it with anyone other than the service providers below.

Service providers

  • Telegram: the channel your messages reach us through and our replies go back on.
  • Anthropic (Claude): the AI model that understands your messages and writes the replies. Your messages are sent to it for processing, which may happen outside Saudi Arabia.

Deleting your data

Send Fazaa "delete my data" and confirm with "yes delete". Your messages, appointments, and all your data with us are permanently deleted. You can start using Fazaa again afterwards.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists, until you delete it.

What we don't do

  • We never ask for passwords or bank details in the chat.
  • Fazaa doesn't send messages to anyone in your name.

Website analytics

We use GoatCounter to count site visitors and the pages they visit. It uses no cookies and collects no personally identifying data.

Contact

For any privacy question, email info@ifazaa.com or message @ifazaa_bot.
