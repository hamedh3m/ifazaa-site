Let Fazaa read your texts

Your spending is tracked on its own, bills and appointments become reminders, and you can ask Fazaa “What texts did I get today?”. A Prime feature.

  • Verification codes are discarded instantly
  • Card and account numbers are wiped first
  • Texts are deleted within a day and a half, and no person ever sees them

Before you start: copy your private link

Open the Fazaa app → “Account” → “Phone texts” → “Copy my link”. The link is private to you; don't share it.

Open Fazaa and copy your link
1

Add the shortcut

Tap the button → “Set Up Shortcut”.

Add the shortcut
2

Paste your link

Tap the “Link” field → “Paste” → “Add Shortcut”.

3

Run it once

Open Shortcuts and tap “Fazaa Messages” once; when asked, choose “Always Allow”. Without this it won't run while your phone is locked.

4

Create the automation

Shortcuts → “Automation” → “+” → “Message”. Change “Sender” to “Message” → “Contains” → type the letter “a”.

5

Run “Fazaa Messages”

Search below for “Run Shortcut” → pick “Fazaa Messages”. The input becomes “Message” on its own.

6

Turn off confirmation

Tap the arrow next to the trigger: keep “Automation” on and “Ask Before Running” off, then save.

7

For Arabic texts

Make a second automation the same way, with the letter “ا” instead of “a”.

1

Download the app

Download SMS to URL Forwarder (a free, open-source APK). If you see a warning, choose “Install anyway”.

2

Allow SMS

Open the app and allow it to receive SMS and notifications.

3

Add a rule

Tap “+”: type * in Sender, paste your link in URL, leave the rest as is, and save.

Done. Try it: have someone text you, then ask Fazaa “What texts did I get today?”.
