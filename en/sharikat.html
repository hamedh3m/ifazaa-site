What it does for your company

A lead assistant that talks to you, and specialist assistants for each team that report to it. The same way we run our own projects.

  • A summary every morning

    Sales, bookings, and important emails from your systems, in one message.

    “Yesterday's sales SAR 12,400, down 8% on last week”
  • Project and team reports

    Weekly and monthly for every team: sales and expenses, project progress, overdue tasks, and notes and suggestions.

    “Give me this week's sales team report”
  • An assistant for every team

    Marketing, finance, operations. Each prepares its report and suggestions, and you approve before anything happens.

    “Marketing: 3 posts ready and waiting for your approval”
  • Task and deadline follow-up

    Reminds each person of their task and deadline, and tells you if something slips.

    “Remind Sarah about the client proposal on Wednesday, and tell me if it's not done”
  • Meeting prep

    An agenda before the meeting, and a summary with action items after.

    “Prepare talking points for Sunday's meeting with the sales team”
  • Emails, proposals, and letters

    Writes the formal wording, and you review it before sending.

    “Write a quote for Al Nukhba for 50 training hours”

These are just examples of what the AI assistant can do for you, and it can do more. Need something else? Tell us and we'll look into it.
