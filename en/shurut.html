Setup terms

What we need from you to set Fazaa up for your business and have it working from day one, on the yearly plan and on a one-time purchase.

What we need from every business

  • A WhatsApp number for the business: a new number just for Fazaa (a new SIM), or your current WhatsApp Business number, which we connect while you keep using the app on it. We need the phone with that number during setup for the verification code.
  • A Meta business account: we help you create it. It needs the business name and address, and usually a commercial registration or freelance document to verify the account.
  • Your business details: prices, hours, location, offers, how booking works, and the questions you get asked a lot. And how you want it to talk to customers: formal or friendly.
  • Your booking or sales system (if you have one): such as Rekaz or similar. We need integration access or a user account so it can read and record bookings.
  • Your number and your staff's numbers: yours so the assistant can talk to you and send your report, and your staff's for the people you want it to follow up with.
  • An hour of your time: we sit down with you once to gather the details, and test with you before it talks to any customer.

Yearly plan

  • No device or payment accounts needed: we run it on our servers, and usage and WhatsApp messages are included in the price.
  • Tell us, or tell Fazaa itself, about any change to your prices or hours, and we update it.
  • Support and updates are included all year.

One-time purchase: the device is at your place

  • A fixed internet connection: a fiber router or fixed home internet at the business. Ideally the device connects to the router by cable.
  • Constant power: an outlet near the router that stays on, since the device runs 24 hours. We recommend a UPS for power cuts.
  • A spot for the device: it's palm-sized and fits on a shelf next to the router, away from heat and humidity.
  • A payment card in your name: for the WhatsApp Business account and the AI account, because monthly usage is billed to your accounts based on your number of conversations.
  • Accounts in your name: we open them for you and they belong to you, and you give us access so we can set up and connect.

The device becomes yours. Damage from breakage, water, or repeated power-offs isn't covered by the warranty.

Yearly support (for one-time purchases)

  • Remote access: with your consent we install a secure support tool on the device, so we can fix and update it without visiting.
  • The device stays on and connected: don't unplug or reset it without talking to us.
  • Your accounts stay active: the payment card on WhatsApp and the AI account must stay valid, or replies to customers will stop.
  • A contact person: you or an authorized employee, whom we can reach if we need something on site.
  • Price: SAR 990 a year, half price for the first 10 subscribers.
  • What it covers: software faults, updates, changes to your business details, and regular checks that everything is running.
  • What it doesn't cover: internet or power outages at your place, device faults from misuse, and usage costs on your accounts.
